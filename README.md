# Climate Physics Model: Anthropogenic Greenhouse Effect

A transparent, data-driven Python notebook that provides verifiable proof of the human-induced greenhouse effect through three core pillars of climate physics.

## Overview

This notebook demonstrates how human activities are driving climate change by integrating three fundamental aspects of climate science:

### 1. **CO2 Balance** 🌍
Verifies the human origin of atmospheric CO2 increase by:
- Tracking atmospheric CO2 concentrations from pre-industrial times to present
- Analyzing the carbon budget (emissions vs. atmospheric increase vs. natural sinks)
- Demonstrating ocean acidification as evidence of CO2 absorption
- Proving mass balance consistency

### 2. **Radiation Balance** ☀️
Applies fundamental physics (Stefan-Boltzmann Law) to calculate:
- Solar radiation input to Earth
- Terrestrial radiation output
- The natural greenhouse effect (+33°C warming)
- Energy trapped by greenhouse gases

### 3. **Energy Balance** 🌡️
Demonstrates warming through radiative forcing:
- Calculating CO2 radiative forcing (logarithmic relationship)
- Determining expected temperature response
- Comparing predictions with observed warming
- Projecting future warming scenarios

## Scientific Foundation

### Data Sources
- **Atmospheric CO2**: Ice core records + Mauna Loa Observatory
- **Carbon Budget**: Global Carbon Project
- **Ocean pH**: Ocean monitoring networks

### Physical Laws Applied
- **Stefan-Boltzmann Law**: E = σT⁴
- **Radiative Forcing**: ΔF = 5.35 × ln(C/C₀)
- **Climate Sensitivity**: ΔT = λ × ΔF

## Verification

The notebook demonstrates three independent lines of evidence that all converge:

1. **Mass Balance**: Human emissions (680 GtC) >> atmospheric increase (305 GtC)  
   ✓ Remainder absorbed by ocean and land sinks

2. **Energy Balance**: CO2 forcing (+2.13 W/m²) × sensitivity (0.8 K/W/m²) = 1.7°C  
   ✓ Matches observed warming of 1.2°C (remaining is committed warming)

3. **Ocean Chemistry**: pH decline consistent with CO2 uptake  
   ✓ Confirms carbon is entering the ocean from atmosphere
